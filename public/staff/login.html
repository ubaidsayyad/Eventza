Welcome, staff.

The Eventza scanner is restricted to authenticated Hatchable project collaborators who have been assigned to an event by an administrator.

Before you start

  1. Ask the Eventza administrator to add your account as a project collaborator in Hatchable.
  2. Ask them to assign your account email to the event you are checking in.
  3. Sign in to your Hatchable collaborator account in this browser.
  4. Open the scanner and grant camera permission when asked.
A URL alone never grants entry-scanning permission. Every scan is checked by a protected backend route.
